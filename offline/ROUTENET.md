# ROUTENET — the precomputed validated route network (v0.18.3.211)

Generated by `offline/gen_routenet.py`; shipped as `src/world_map_routenet.inl`
(data section regenerated with `--emit`, runtime section below the marker is
handwritten). Companion diagnosis: BAT208 section at the bottom of this file.

## Why a network (the .208 verdict)

The .203/.205/.207/.208 BATs all ground at the Galbadia pass because of GRID
QUANTIZATION: the pass's only reliable line is **z = −25086 ± 8 through
x ∈ [−44700, −44540]** (a 192u-wide corridor, ~96–98u clearance per side), and
that line lies BETWEEN 128u grid rows. Every grid plan put the route row at
z=−25024 — 88u from the ~1u sliver wall at z=−24936, inside the engine's sticky
wall-foot zone — and no executor-level fix (G4 8u centerline probes, ABEAM
advance, WPSKIP/retreat escalation) could hold a line the route itself cannot
express. The .208 log: 22 s of camera-write steering on that row moved the
character ZERO units west (x pinned in [−44515, −44446]).

The structural fix: routes between known locations are EXACT POLYLINES with
arbitrary coordinates, computed and validated offline, spliced into
`s_drivePathWX/WY` at plan time. The executor already follows arbitrary
polylines tightly (advance 64u + ABEAM 144u).

## The .210 verdict: THE PASS IS IMPASSABLE LIVE (v0.18.3.211)

The .209 network shipped the pass as an exact max-clearance polyline
(z=−25088 line, 96–98u clearance vs the 90u sticky-zone radius — 6–8u of
modeled margin). The .210 BAT ran it live (Logs session 14:27:15,
v0.18.3.210): the transit fix worked (clean chain Station→EastPlains→
bypass→WestPlains→Timber, zero field entries), but the drive cycled
**26–29 km from Timber and never crossed the neck**, riding the validated
polyline. The corrected replica (`EngineSimC(hatch="away")`) had predicted
the fail. Verdict: 6–8u of margin is not enough for the live engine; the
pass edge is REMOVED from the tables (its polyline is preserved in
`routenet_state.json` under `"GG West Plains|Timber"` for the record).

Two more corridors were retired in .211, both for measured reasons:

* **GG East Plains ↔ Dollet (the .205 gap track)**: deterministically
  sim-blocks BOTH directions at (−31.3k, −26.2k) — root-caused this session:
  the massif-tip gap is roofed by steep walkable "skirt" triangles (block
  (48,60) polys #80/#81, footprints x[−31744,−31210] z[−26624,−26414] and
  x[−31246,−30720] z[−26414,−25926]); the engine's 8-entry MRU triangle
  cache answers standing/validator queries with the skirt surface (~200–400u
  above the true ground), tripping the |ΔH|≥200 step gate in open land.
  8 lattice-repair rounds and a 6×3 sweep of alternate crossing lines all
  re-block. Live it was crossed exactly once at full speed (.205), but it
  blocked 4 matrix pairs in .209/.210 and is redundant now — removed.
* **The Timber–Dollet ROAD canyon neck**: the mod's `[ROADV:TIMBER-DOLLET]`
  diagnostic maps a 46-triangle road centerline Timber(−22564,−4867)→Dollet.
  Seeding an edge from it validates fine EXCEPT the Yaulny canyon neck at
  ~(−30040,−20000), which is a **~64u-wide walkable sliver** in the engine
  collision — deep inside the 90u sticky zone, narrower than the pass we
  just proved impassable. Not shipped. The shipped corridor parallels the
  road on the Timber approach (x≈−30.1k…−23k) but crosses the mountain belt
  at the proven far-north diagonal (z≈−15.7k→−13.1k).

## Generation pipeline (per edge)

1. **Seed**: `Planner2` (fitted gate, 32u sub-march, ×4 near-wall cost) on the
   32u walk grids; fallbacks: terrain-rule (byte13) A* — the engine's
   walkable-preferred scan crosses overlay spots the bit7 planner refuses —
   and finer center-sampled A*. Explicit seeds (`seed_hint`): the
   junction↔junction GG bypass (spliced Station corridors), the .211 GG East
   Plains↔Yaulny Plains link, and the three .211 splits of the validated
   Dollet↔Timber polyline.
2. **Refine**: every vertex is hill-climbed along the local perpendicular onto
   the MAXIMUM-CLEARANCE channel (distance-to-nonwalk maximized, target 160u,
   window ±224u, continuity clamp).
3. **Resample**: 32u spacing through pinches (clearance < 98u), 96u elsewhere;
   each emitted point micro-re-centered ±24u.
4. **Area avoidance**: refinement never places a vertex inside a decoded
   firing-area bbox (`s_entryAims`) of a NON-endpoint location (+64u margin).
5. **Validate**: `run_drive3` (cache-capture engine, hatch="away", full
   G-suite, swept probes) drives the polyline in BOTH directions on the exact
   oracle; arrival = within 560u of the node; edge heads inside sticky gate
   mouths are trimmed. One resume-with-learned-overlay is allowed per
   direction (the live executor replans around a transient capture block and
   continues; a REAL grind re-blocks at the same spot and still fails).
6. **Repair**: a validation block triggers a local 16u lattice Dijkstra
   (depth-penalized) that replaces the ±1300u section around the block; up to
   8 rounds.

## Runtime (src/world_map_routenet.inl)

UNCHANGED in v211 (data-only release). `RouteNetPlan(startX, startY)` is
tried FIRST by `PlanDrivePath`: nearest edge vertex to start and to
`s_driveTarget` (≤2048u each, else decline) → node-graph Dijkstra with the
.210 HARD TRANSIT RULE (junctions + at-mouth seeded starts + the target node
only) → local hops on/off the network → splice into `s_drivePathWX/WY`.
Logs `[ROUTENET] plan ok: ...` with the via-edge chain, or
`[ROUTENET] declined: <reason>`. Cap: 4 network plans per drive, then grid
fallback. Declines NEVER break anything — grid A* runs exactly as before.

## Known limitations / risks

* The east corridor is ~32 km GG→Timber (was 53 km on paper through the
  pass): LONG drives, several minutes of cruising. The .203 pause/resume,
  stall escalation and the 4-plan cap + grid fallback own any live grind.
* The Yaulny Plains belt crossing (z≈−15.7k→−13.1k diagonal) is sim-proven
  in both directions as part of the old Dollet↔Timber edge AND as the v211
  splits, but has never been driven live — it is the experiment of the .211
  BAT. It is a wide-clearance channel (no sub-98u pinch on the line), unlike
  the pass.
* 6 of 20 matrix pairs use one learned-overlay resume on the long hauls
  (transient capture blocks, position moves on after replan). Live
  equivalent: one `[ROUTENET]` replan mid-drive; not a grind signature.
* Node mouths (gate wedges) are excluded from edge validation (first/last
  ~640u); entries remain the job of the .206 machinery.
* Local hops assume the character starts ≤2km from the network; drives that
  start mid-wilderness fall back to grid A* (expected, logged).

## Engine-replica update (BAT208 refit, in nav_sim.py)

`EngineSimC(hatch="away")` — the deterministic escape hatch (1 step per 23
rejected attempts) only grants steps that strictly INCREASE the distance
to the offending non-walkable geometry while inside the sticky zone
(depth ≤ ZONE_R = 90u). Fit: the .208 grind (zero parallel-to-wall progress,
fan escapes away from the wall DID move). Replication on the logged .208
route (`outputs/ff8/bat208/`): old model (`hatch="free"`) ARRIVES at Timber by
crawling the bad row — the blind spot that let .204–.208 validate offline;
new model BLOCKS at the neck at idx 106 vs live idx 103–105 — and the .210
live BAT confirmed the corrected model's pass verdict. The executor sim
(`run_drive3`) also carries the .208 ABEAM advance and the .207 stall
escalation for parity with the shipped executor.
